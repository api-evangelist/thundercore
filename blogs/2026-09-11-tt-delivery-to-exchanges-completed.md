We would like to share an update with our community: ThunderCore completed the delivery of the corresponding BEP20-TT for TT held by exchanges by September 2, 2026, and all relevant exchanges confirmed receipt by September 3, 2026. We have also received questions and concerns from users whose exchanges do not yet support BEP20-TT withdrawals. We understand how important access to your TT is, and we have relayed relevant user questions and feedback to the respective exchanges for their attention.
